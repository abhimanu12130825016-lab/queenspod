The Queen's Pod experience

Arrive for the view.
Stay for the feeling.

Set beside the river in the hills of Gangtok, Queen's Pod is a place to slow down — a garden of warm lights, generous plates and conversations that last a little longer.

The architecture folds around the landscape. Bamboo, glass, stone and plants make the room feel both hidden and open. Come for lunch, an evening out, a celebration or simply the pleasure of finding somewhere new.
